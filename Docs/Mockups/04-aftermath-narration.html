T. Valerius · Decanus
The Aftermath
Day 1 · clock paused

The Seventeenth, the Eighteenth, the Nineteenth. Three eagles, Centurion, and I watched all three go under. Whatever is left of Varus's army is lying in that mud behind us.

The bulk of them have moved on. Arminius has bigger game than a handful of us: they are hunting the other columns that broke east, toward the river, and they will not turn back for six men.

Which leaves the looters. They are already in among the dead, stripping mail and cutting throats where they find breath, and they will pick off stragglers one at a time. There are men alive in these woods, sir. Let us go and find them before the looters do.
