Century

UI mockups · throwaway HTML · not production code
01
Title Screen
CENTURY in the rain-soaked clearing: free-floating links with the centre-growing underline, and the three-slot LOAD SAVE overlay (click LOAD SAVE to open it).
02
Opening Sequence
The scripted wake-up in the rain: edge blur, mid-blink bars, a guide chevron over the Centurion, the subtitle band and the STEALTH controls-only sheet that holds time.
03
Overmap HUD
The v2 overmap layout: top nav strip, boxed clock, objective, EVENTS + CONTACT, single bottom bar with identity, resources, morale, column metrics and CAMP/STEALTH pills.
04
The Aftermath
The guided first overmap area: Valerius's narration modal over the leashed battle ground, linear objectives, story POI markers and six men in a compact bottom bar.
05
POI Event
The event pop-up at a point of interest: an art band, prose, three choice rows with mechanics lines (one hovered) and the WHO IS WATCHING officer strip.
06
Scout Report
OPPORTUNITY SIGHTED: the Speculator's report after the night's rest, with intel quality in words, three tallies, a risk line and MARK IT ON THE MAP / LET IT LIE.
07
Battle HUD
Mid-battle: timer and time controls, engagement / officers / combat log column, per-man contubernium dots, order bar with key caps and the formation picker, Centurion status, squad inspect on hover.
08
Deployment
The pre-battle sheet: choose which contubernia take the field, read the baked tacmap, place the line on the gold ground and pick where reserves enter.
09
Battle Summary
The post-battle sheet over the darkened field: the fallen, the spoils, and what the Centurion, the offices and the men took from the fight.
10
Camp — The Century
The organisation chart: command band with the six offices (Signifer vacant), then the five contubernia with rich soldier cards, tier discs and status chips.
11
Camp — Rest & Orders
The camp's REST & ORDERS tab: two rest options with their projected effects, the day's watch strip, and the orders a camp can issue.
12
Camp — Crafting & Stations
The camp's CRAFTING tab (recipe cards with inputs, stock and CRAFT ALL) and its CAMP STATIONS tab (six station cards with level pips, effects and build costs).
13
Soldier Sheet
The soldier detail modal for Publius Naso (Contubernium III) over a faint org chart on THE CENTURY tab: tier strip, attitude, bars as words, record, bonds and grudges.
14
The Office
THE OFFICE overlay on THE CENTURY tab: two office cards side by side — the Optio with points to invest on a vertical tradition track, and the vacant Signifer whose track learns nothing.
15
Inventory
The INVENTORY overlay: subtype sections as 8-column tile grids led by a food ledger tile, count badges, one tile selected and inspected in the footer beside the STORAGE readout.
16
Army
The ARMY drill-down: office cards for the establishment and five squad cards on the root page, a breadcrumb back-trail, and a Contubernium III page listing its eight men.
17
Commander
The COMMANDER screen: identity line and level bar for Marcus Cornelius, a three-card doctrine offer tinted by philosophy, and the doctrines already held in three columns.
18
Journal
A proposed Journal overlay: the march as a day-by-day, watch-by-watch record so the story stays legible in hindsight.
19
Encyclopedia
A proposed Encyclopedia overlay: every rule of the sim stated as mechanics, with the Formations entry open.
Every page scales a 1920×1080 stage to your window. Press N on any page to toggle designer notes. Tabs and pills that switch panels are clickable; everything else is static.
