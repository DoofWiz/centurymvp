Formations
A contubernium holds one formation at a time. The selected squad cycles through the five below in the order shown. A formation sets three things: how much of a frontal blow is blocked, how fast the squad moves, and how fast the men tire. Changing formation takes a few seconds; while it re-forms the squad neither blocks nor moves. Wounded men keep their place. An empty bunk leaves a gap.
Line1 of 5
Blocks frontal blows. Normal speed.
Flanks are open. The default on deployment.
1Cycle
Testudo2 of 5
Blocks most frontal blows. Slow.
Men tire faster. Blocks flank blows in part.
1Cycle
Double line3 of 5
The rear rank relieves the front as it tires.
Holds longer. Covers half the ground of Line.
1Cycle
Loose4 of 5
Hardly blocks. Fast.
Used to skirmish and to run. Pila loose sooner.
1Cycle
Column5 of 5
Moves fastest. Only the head fights.
Breaks easily if struck. The march formation on contact.
1Cycle
What breaks a formation
◆Entering forest or a ford drops any formation to Loose until the squad is clear of it.
◆A blow from the flank ignores the block of Line and Double line. Only Testudo blocks it in part.
◆A squad that loses its decanus re-forms slower until a man is appointed in camp.
◆A squad at Breaking or below cannot enter Testudo. A Broken squad holds no formation at all.
◆Stamina drains at the formation's rate while the squad moves, faster still on a slope.
◆Orders given to a squad mid-change wait until it has re-formed.
