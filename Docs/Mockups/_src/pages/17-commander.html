Doctrines are the only lever on how the men see you. What they see decides which offers come and how officers react at points of interest.
The XP bar carries no number; TO LEVEL 4 is the whole readout. The coloured words in the identity line reuse the card tints (gold Roman, olive Survivor, blood Barbarian).
Percentages are never shown in-game. The identity line is the only readout of the three hidden philosophy scores; it rewrites itself when the lead changes or a second philosophy pulls close ("...with a Survivor's caution").
Offers are weighted by the hidden scores: a Roman-leaning commander draws Roman cards more often. One of each is the neutral draw shown here; two Roman and one other is the common case at this state. Effects state mechanics only, no numbers.
