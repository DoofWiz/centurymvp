Point of interest · Battle site
A Wasteland of Corpses

The column halts at the edge of the trees and nobody speaks. What was the marching camp of three legions lies open along the track for a mile: mail-shirts turned inside out, the wagons burnt to the axles, and the dead lying where the line broke, three deep in places. The crows have had two days of it.

Balbus finds the stump of a signum with the cloth cut away. Somewhere in this there is kit worth carrying and coin sewn into belts, and somewhere in this there are the men who are stripping it, and they are not far off.
