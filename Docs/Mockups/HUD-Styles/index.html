Battle HUD — five art styles

Same screen, same data, five visual identities. Pure HTML/CSS, no build step — open any card.

01 · Parchment & Wax

Campaign-ledger look: aged parchment panels, sepia ink, wax-seal accents, hand-drawn map field. Total War energy.

02 · Imperial Marble

Monumental Rome: pale carved-stone slabs, chiseled lettering, laurel and meander motifs, purple-and-bronze inlay.

03 · Modern Minimal

Frameless contemporary restraint: translucent smoke panels, hairline bars, two accent colours, diagrammatic field.

04 · Iron & Blood

Grimdark weight: riveted gunmetal plates, cut corners, arterial red, ember glows, mud-and-dusk battlefield.

05 · Pompeii Fresco

Flat painted plaster: Pompeiian red, ochre and charcoal fields, Greek-key borders, mural-style illustrated field.
